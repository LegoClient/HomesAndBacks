HomesAndBacks Commands
======================

/sethome [name]   - Save your current location as a home (default name: home)
/home [name]      - Teleport to a saved home (default: home)
/delhome [name]   - Delete a saved home (default: home)
/homes            - List all your saved homes
/back             - Return to your location before the last teleport
                    (use /back again to toggle between the two spots)
